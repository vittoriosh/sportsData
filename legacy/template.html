Did the money help?

Pick how many seasons of spending to add up and the season they end in. We compare each club's points the season before the spending started with its points in the final season, and see whether the biggest spenders improved more than everyone else.

Club by club

Blue bars show net spending (money paid minus money received), green or red bars show the change in league points. Verdicts: Helped means an above-median spender gained 8 or more points. Did not help means an above-median spender lost 8 or more. Clubs that spent less are labelled by what happened to them. Everything in between is little change.

Spending rank against finishing rank

One row per club, biggest spender at the top. The grey dot is where the club ranks for net spending that season, the blue dot is where it finished. A green line means it finished higher than its spending suggests, red means lower.

Do big spenders finish higher?

Every season from 2007-08 to 2022-23, clubs are grouped by spending that season (64 club-seasons per group), then we look at how each group finished. Net spending ranks some big clubs low because they also sell a lot, so gross spending is the better measure of how much a club invests.
